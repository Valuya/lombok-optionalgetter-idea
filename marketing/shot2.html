OptionalGetter for Lombok
What it restores in the editor for @OptionalGetter-generated accessors
✎
Code completion
getEmailOptional() is suggested on any field annotated @OptionalGetter.
🔍
Find Usages
Find Usages on the field also finds the generated getter's call sites, not just direct field references.
↪
Go to declaration
Ctrl-click / Ctrl-B on a getXxxOptional() call jumps to the annotated field.
Runs alongside the bundled Lombok plugin. Pairs with io.github.valuya:lombok-optionalgetter-overlay on Maven Central.
